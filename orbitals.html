What changed from Bohr

Bohr put the electron on a ring at a fixed distance, like a planet. It has no path at all. The cloud is a map of where it is likely to be, and nothing is orbiting anything.

Why two per orbital

An orbital is one standing wave, and only two electrons fit in it — one spin up, one spin down. A third has nowhere to go, so the next electron starts a new orbital.

Why they spread out first

Electrons repel, so within a set of same-energy orbitals each one takes an empty orbital before any of them doubles up. That is why 2p fills ↑ ↑ ↑ before ↑↓ ↑↓ ↑↓.
